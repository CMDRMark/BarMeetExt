# Privacy Policy — BarMeetExt (BarMeet)

**Last updated:** 20 October 2026

This policy describes how the **BarMeet** Chrome extension (Google OAuth application name: **BarMeetExt**) handles information. This document is public; no sign-in is required.

## Who we are

BarMeet is a Chrome extension. Use the developer contact email on the Chrome Web Store listing or the Google Cloud OAuth consent screen for privacy questions.

## Summary

- No BarMeet servers store your calendar data.
- No selling or advertising use of your data.
- Calendar access only after you connect inside the extension.
- Preferences stay in Chrome extension storage on your device.

## Google API scope

`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar` — read today’s events, create Meet/Focus events, update/delete when you request, read join links. No Gmail, Drive, or other Google APIs.

## Data used

| Data | Purpose | Location |
|------|---------|----------|
| Google account (OAuth) | Connection state, sign-out | Extension only |
| Calendar events | UI, reminders, actions you request | From Google API; not uploaded to our servers |
| OAuth tokens | Calendar API auth | Chrome Identity on device |
| Settings | Preferences | `chrome.storage` on device |

## Retention

No calendar database on our side. Tokens until sign-out, revoke, or uninstall.

## We do not collect

Passwords, payments, health data, browsing history, keystroke tracking, or arbitrary page content from sites you visit.

## Sharing

No sale of data. Calendar traffic goes to Google under your consent. Limited Use per [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy).

## Your choices

Sign out in the extension, uninstall, or revoke at [Google third-party access](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Children

Not directed at children under 13.

## Contact

Developer email on Chrome Web Store / OAuth consent screen.
