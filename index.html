Privacy Policy — BarMeet

Last updated: 27 July 2026

BarMeet is a Chrome extension that helps you see current and upcoming Google Calendar meetings and start a Google Meet link from your browser.

Summary

BarMeet does not operate its own servers that store your calendar data. It does not sell your data. Access to Google Calendar happens through Google’s APIs after you sign in; preferences stay in your browser.

Data we access or process

When you choose Connect Google Calendar, BarMeet asks Google for permission to access your calendar. With your consent, the extension may access:

  • Your Google account identity as provided by Chrome Identity / Google OAuth (so you can stay signed in)
  • Events on your primary Google Calendar (titles, times, conference/join links, and related event fields needed to list, join, create, update, or delete meetings you request)
  • OAuth access tokens managed by Chrome Identity (cached by Chrome; not uploaded to a BarMeet backend)

When you use the extension, it may:

  • Create a “Quick Meeting” with a Google Meet link, or a “Focus Time” event
  • Update or delete calendar events you choose to reschedule or cancel
  • Copy a Meet join link to your clipboard when you start a meeting
  • Show optional local notifications (meeting reminders / focus end) if you enable them

Data stored on your device

BarMeet stores preferences in Chrome’s storage (for example: meeting and focus durations, whether reminders and cancel confirmation are enabled). These settings are not calendar contents.

BarMeet does not maintain a separate cloud database of your meetings.

Data we do not collect

BarMeet does not intentionally collect or upload:

  • Passwords
  • Payment or health information
  • Your general browsing history
  • Keystrokes, mouse tracking, or similar activity monitoring
  • Website page content from sites you visit

Sharing

BarMeet does not sell or rent user data. Calendar and account data are requested from Google under Google’s terms and your OAuth consent. We do not transfer that data to other third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes.

Third-party services

Authentication and calendar features use Google (Google Identity / OAuth and Google Calendar API). Google’s handling of data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Disconnect: use Sign out in the extension popup, or remove the extension in Chrome
  • Revoke access: visit your Google Account third-party access settings and remove BarMeet / the OAuth app
  • Notifications and reminders can be turned off in the extension settings

Children’s privacy

BarMeet is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the extension’s behavior changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions about BarMeet, contact the publisher email listed on the Chrome Web Store listing for this extension.
